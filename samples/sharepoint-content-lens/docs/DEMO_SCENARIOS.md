# Publisher demos

Open **Page → Try it with an example page** or **Comments → Try example discussions**, choose a scenario, then select **Check page** or **Review comments**. The examples are fictional and their results are prepared. They make no SharePoint or Copilot request. In **Compare**, **Try it with an example page** shows a prepared comparison of two fictional pages.

| Tab | Scenario | What to look at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page | 1 · Make a move notice easier to act on | An ambiguous date, reader-blaming wording and two writing rules; separate heading, link and long-paragraph checks |
| Page | 2 · Keep a policy announcement constructive | Reasoned criticism beside a hostile instruction; separate news-summary and vague-link checks |
| Page | 3 · Know what a clean result does not prove | No wording flags, but an image and a contact card that the review does not cover |
| Comments | 1 · Separate criticism from a personal attack | "Only an idiot" beside a legitimate request for evidence |
| Comments | 2 · Read frustration and replies in context | A dismissive reply, frustration with a form, possible sarcasm |
| Comments | 3 · Keep a legitimate support request visible | A request for confidential health advice that is not flagged |
