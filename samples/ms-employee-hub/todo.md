# Microsoft Employee Hub | implementation tracker

**Objective:** build one SPFx sample where an employee or leader can ask a specific question in Copilot, edit the answer inline, expand into the right workspace without starting over, and explicitly complete a simulated action. SharePoint, Teams and the Cowork plug-in expose the same capabilities and synthetic records, not four implementations.

**Current checkpoint: local SLT validation candidate; not cleared for a tenant rollout or live keynote.** Use the [rehearsal scripts and package links](README.md#slt-rehearsal-material). The compiled checks load actual SPFx exports using simulated callbacks; only an authorized signed-in host can establish real Copilot routing, installation, focus and discovery.

## Full-screen UX: design the workspace, not a page of parts

**Visual reference:** [the Microsoft portals in `Q:\git\spfx-full-screen-poc`][poc], reviewed at `a26dd46`. Its three React portals were inspected at 1280 and 390 px. Use their typography, photographic crops, whitespace, section hierarchy and responsive behavior as the Microsoft design reference. Zava One Hub remains the reference for host pairing and personal-app packaging. Neither reference proves this sample runs in a tenant.

| Workspace | First impression and what the extra space does |
|---|---|
| [Microsoft home / portal][poc-home] | A magazine front page: a large editorial headline beside a tilted campaign photograph and circular portrait inset. A slim briefing strip leads into an asymmetric story spread with a quieter personal rail. On a phone, the headline and reading action come before the collage. |
| [HRweb][poc-hr] | A personal welcome: blue arched portrait, layered photo and one next-step action. Below it, learning leads; the quieter rewards and time-away sections respect privacy. Expanding a leave draft skips the hero and uses the width for the editable calendar, entitlement and eligible coverage. |
| [TechWeb][poc-tech] | A service front door: direct help action opposite a human portrait, then a service notice and active conversations beside the illustrated device. Expanding a ticket puts the draft beside the selected device and guide, not inside a larger copy of the intake card. |
| [Business SLT][slt] | An operating review, not a fourth POC portal: lead with one finding, a broad market map and aligned gap ranking. When a lead ribbon is expanded, keep the selected cohort in focus and use the extra space for channel comparison, exact counts and the evidence-backed action. |

**Implementation rule:** compose a dedicated landing and focused-detail layout for each workspace. Reuse typed state, commands, accessible controls and chart primitives; do not mount web parts inside web parts or render a list of complete Copilot cards. Inline answers one question. Expand goes straight to the same selection or draft with useful surrounding context; opening a workspace directly goes to its home. Use the same composition within each host's available width. Leave outer navigation, Close/Back and chat to their host; do not port the POC's launcher/dialog, suite-bar overrides, hardcoded sister-site links, session storage or unsupported HRweb Copilot panel.
