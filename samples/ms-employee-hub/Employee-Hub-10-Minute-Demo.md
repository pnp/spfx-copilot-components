# Ten minutes: an operating review and a working day

**Audience:** leadership, HR and IT service owners. **Setup:** start the [offline studio](README.md#review-it-locally), reset, choose the rehearsal lead and `2026-11-27T09:00:00Z`. Use separate clean conversations for separate asks. No employee data, real service tickets or real meetings.

| Time | Prompt, expected component and presenter action | Decision or boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-2:00 | **“How are EMEA bookings tracking against plan?”** → `CompareSalesPerformance` (#08, `region=EMEA`). Inspect weighted regional attainment. **“Why did Germany's quarter-end outlook move?”** → `ExplainForecastMovement` (#07, `marketId=DE`). Inspect the signed slippage bar and its two reporting cuts. | Actual bookings versus phased target is a different question from the estimated finish versus the previous estimate. Don't add the figures. |
| 2:00-4:00 | **“How many partner leads in Germany became opportunities this quarter?”** → `TraceLeadConversion` (#09, `DE`, `partner`, `current-quarter`). Compare 8/60 and 12/40; restore the partner ribbon, expand and hand off to **Prepare an investigation**. Review owner, checkpoint and citation; confirm one session action (#06). | Call out the denominator and date. The action does not change commercial history. |
| 4:00-5:30 | **“Find a step-free room in London for six.”** → `BookMeetingRoom` (#04, `officeId=LON`, `capacity=6`, `accessible=true`). Search, choose a room, expand, review, then confirm. Show the receipt and rerun the same slot. | Searching and selecting are not reservations. Confirmation changes availability only in this synthetic session. |
| 5:30-7:15 | **“Prepare an annual leave request.”** → `RequestTimeOff` (#15, `leaveType=annual`). Select dates and inspect the working days and projected balance before confirming. In a separate reviewer fixture ask **“Which leave requests need my decision?”** → `ReviewTimeOffRequest` (#17). Open a seeded colleague, compare the portrait with the queue, review coverage, confirm and see the updated status. | The reviewer is not approving their own leave or a record synchronized from another instance. Notes remain private. |
| 7:15-8:30 | **“Does my assigned laptop need attention?”** → `InspectMyDevice` (#19, laptop). Switch between the illustrated laptop and headset. **“What is happening with IT-2048?”** → `TrackMyITTicket` (#23, `ticketId=IT-2048`). Inspect its next owner without opening a new intake. | An existing personal thread is not a shared outage, a new ticket or an equipment purchase. |
| 8:30-10:00 | **“Open the employee hub on TechWeb.”** → `OpenEmployeeHub` (#30, `landing=tech`). Expand to TechWeb. Compare with Microsoft home, HRweb and Business in the all-up app. In a fixed workspace, show that other workspaces cannot be selected. **“What can this hub help me do?”** → `ExploreHubCapabilities` (#29). Copy an example without executing it. | Five Teams packages, not five SharePoint landing pages. Don't present local tabs as authenticated Teams. |
