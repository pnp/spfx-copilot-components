# ZavaCore Procurement Command Center - designer and rehearsal review

## Experience goal

The sample should feel like a global trade atelier inside Copilot: material, precise, spatial, and
transparent. It must not look like a generic AI dashboard, requisition form, or collection of equal KPI
cards. The first frame names the decision, evidence, accountable role, and next human action.

## Visual language

- Host-provided Fluent tokens establish brand and operational structure; semantic risk and success
  colors always have text/table equivalents.
- Compact context strips identify the task; avoid decorative background blobs.
- Analytical regions are wide and unframed or singly bounded. Cards represent real records and forms.
- Financial values use tabular figures; headings remain compact inside operational surfaces.
- D3 geometry answers a decision question and always has exact-value and accessible alternatives.
- Official ZavaCore orange/magenta, charcoal navigation, warm neutral analytical surfaces, field
  photography and named team portraits establish the brand without duplicating another sample's
  palette. High-contrast foreground/button variants live in the Fluent theme boundary.

## Interaction and accessibility review

- Risk weight materially changes supplier geometry and ranking.
- Split percentage changes cost, blended risk, resilience share, and timing confidence.
- Every SVG has role, title, description, visible labels, and an exact table/list equivalent.
- Interactive chart marks support Enter and Space; semantic states include text and icon meaning.
- Navigation and controls retain visible focus; 390 px has no masked clipping or horizontal overflow.
- Reduced motion renders final geometry without delaying information.
- Inspect `ux-review/evidence/zavacore/` for the submission, budget review/receipt, processed inbox,
  award review, exact award-to-investment consequence, region drill-down and Reset. Review 340, 760
  and 1920 px plus the clearly labeled Chromium page-scale captures. Page-scale evidence is not an
  authenticated/native-browser zoom claim.
- Scope is visible: the dashboard's regional analysis does not silently overwrite a focused task's
  prompt filters or draft. Fresh intake approval is separate from the curated sourcing event.
